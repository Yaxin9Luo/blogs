## Writing Workflow

1. Start with the skeleton: context, problem, core judgment, evidence, and reflection.
2. Expand the body: fill in the background and make key concepts explicit.
3. Maintain the note: update tags, status, links, and next questions.

Treat each article as an evolving engineering artifact: `seed` is a raw idea, `active` is in progress, `polished` is ready to share, and `archived` is temporarily frozen.
