Research notes should be organized by problem, not by time. Once a topic becomes mature, scattered reading notes can be upgraded into a survey or project document.

## Recommended Structure

- **Problem**: What problem is the paper or technique solving?
- **Core Claim**: What is the most important claim?
- **Mechanism**: Why might the method work?
- **Limitation**: Which parts should not be accepted uncritically?
- **Relation**: How does it connect to my own research, projects, or internship experience?

## Starting Topics

- Multimodal large language models
- Document understanding and generation
- Design automation and editable artifacts
- Evaluation, feedback, and automatic repair loops
