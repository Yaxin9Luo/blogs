Internship reflections should be organized by project and capability, not only by time. This makes the material reusable later for resumes, interviews, applications, and personal summaries.

<Insight title="Retrospective Focus" tone="reflection">
Do not only write what I did. Write how I understood the problem at first, why that understanding changed, and where the change can be reused later.
</Insight>

## Internship Article Structure

- Project background
- My responsibility boundary
- Technical plan and tradeoffs
- The most important pitfalls
- Collaboration with mentors and teammates
- Updates to my own capability model
