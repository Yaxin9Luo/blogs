This section contains writing that does not directly belong to papers or projects, such as research taste, career judgment, writing methods, and long-term plans.

## Writing Principles

- Support judgments with concrete experience.
- Make uncertainty explicit.
- Do not rush to conclude every problem.
- Leave at least one future-verifiable question in each essay.
